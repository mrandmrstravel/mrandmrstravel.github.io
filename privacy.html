1. Introduction

Mr & Mrs Travel (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website, contact us, request travel services, make a booking, submit payment information, create or use a customer account, or request visa assistance.

Our services include flight bookings, hotel reservations, tour packages, and visa assistance.

We aim to handle your personal information transparently and responsibly, in accordance with applicable laws and regulations in the United Arab Emirates (UAE).

By using our website or providing information to us, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular activity, we will seek that consent separately. This Policy does not replace any consent or authorization required by law.

If you do not agree with this Policy, please consider carefully before submitting personal information through our website or to us directly.

3. Personal Information We Collect

Depending on the services you request and the features you use, we may collect the following categories of information.

3.1 Personal identification information

  • Full name.
  • Date of birth.
  • Nationality.
  • Gender, where required for a booking or application.
  • Passport details, including passport number, issue date, expiry date, and issuing country.
  • Emirates ID or other identification details, where necessary and lawfully requested.

3.2 Contact information

  • Email address.
  • Mobile or telephone number.
  • Residential or correspondence address, where needed.
  • Emergency contact details, where relevant to a requested service.

3.3 Travel and booking information

  • Flight preferences, itinerary, and booking references.
  • Departure and arrival dates and destinations.
  • Hotel reservations and accommodation preferences.
  • Tour package selections and travel arrangements.
  • Details of other travellers included in a booking.
  • Special travel requests that you choose to provide.
  • Booking confirmations, amendments, cancellations, and service history.

3.4 Visa and immigration-related information

When you request visa assistance, we may collect documents and information required for the relevant application, which may include:

  • Passport copies and photographs.
  • Visa application forms and supporting documents.
  • Travel plans and accommodation details.
  • Employment, financial, or sponsorship documents where required for the application.
  • Previous visa details or immigration history, where relevant and lawfully requested.
  • Information about accompanying family members or other applicants.

Visa requirements vary by destination and application type. We will seek to collect information relevant to the service requested.

Please submit sensitive documents only through a designated and appropriate channel. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information or documents unrelated to your request.

3.5 Customer account information

If customer account functionality is provided, we may process:

  • Account name and contact details.
  • Login identifiers and securely protected authentication information.
  • Booking history and service requests.
  • Account preferences and communication settings.
  • Information submitted through your account.

We do not intend to request your account password through ordinary email or messaging.

3.6 Payment and transaction information

Our stated payment method is bank transfer, with manual payment verification. We may collect:

  • Payment amount and date.
  • Bank transfer receipt or payment proof.
  • Transaction reference number.
  • Payer name and relevant payment confirmation details.
  • Booking or invoice reference.
  • Payment status, outstanding balance, refunds, and payment history.

We may use these details to verify payments, reconcile transactions, issue confirmations, and maintain business records.

Do not send us your online banking password, PIN, one-time password (OTP), or full payment card security credentials.

3.7 Communications and enquiries

We may collect information contained in your emails, contact forms, customer support requests, feedback, complaints, and other communications with us.

3.8 Technical information

Depending on the website hosting, security, and other technologies used, technical information may be collected, such as:

  • IP address.
  • Browser and device information.
  • Operating system.
  • Date and time of access.
  • Pages visited and basic website activity.
  • Security and diagnostic logs.

Our website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers when you open a page.

4. How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information for the following purposes, where permitted by applicable law:

  1. Providing travel services: To arrange flights, hotels, tour packages, and related travel services.
  2. Providing visa assistance: To prepare, organise, review, or submit application materials when authorised and appropriate.
  3. Managing bookings: To confirm reservations, manage changes, communicate itinerary details, and provide customer support.
  4. Managing customer accounts: To enable account access and display relevant booking or service history, where those features are available.
  5. Verifying payments: To confirm bank transfers, match transaction references to bookings, and update payment records.
  6. Communicating with customers: To respond to enquiries, request missing documents, provide service updates, and notify you of relevant changes.
  7. Maintaining records: To manage invoices, accounting records, disputes, refunds, and other legitimate business requirements.
  8. Security and fraud prevention: To help identify suspicious transactions, unauthorised access, misuse, or fraudulent activity.
  9. Improving our services: To assess service performance and improve our website and customer experience, where applicable.
  10. Marketing: To send travel offers, promotions, package announcements, and other marketing communications where legally permitted and, where required, with your consent.
  11. Legal compliance: To respond to lawful requests and comply with applicable legal, regulatory, accounting, or other obligations.

We will not intentionally use personal information for purposes that are incompatible with the purposes explained in this Policy without an appropriate legal basis or additional notice where required.

6. Sharing Personal Information with Third Parties

We may share relevant personal information with third parties when reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, complete a transaction, maintain our systems, or comply with applicable law.

6.1 Airlines and travel booking providers

We may provide relevant traveller and itinerary information to airlines, airline booking platforms, ticketing providers, consolidators, or other booking partners to arrange, confirm, change, or cancel flight reservations.

6.2 Hotels and accommodation providers

We may share relevant traveller, reservation, contact, and accommodation details with hotels and other accommodation providers to complete and manage reservations.

6.3 Tour operators and travel service providers

We may disclose relevant information to tour operators, destination service providers, transport providers, or other suppliers involved in a package or travel arrangement.

6.4 Visa service providers and relevant authorities

Where visa assistance is requested, we may share documents and application details with authorised visa processing partners, application centres, embassies, consulates, immigration authorities, or other relevant recipients, as appropriate and legally permitted.

A visa decision is made by the relevant government or immigration authority, not by Mr & Mrs Travel.

6.5 Payment and banking providers

We may share necessary transaction information with banks or payment-related service providers when required to verify a transfer, investigate a payment discrepancy, process a refund, or reconcile a transaction.

6.7 Professional advisers and authorities

We may disclose relevant information to legal, accounting, or professional advisers and to competent authorities where reasonably necessary for legitimate business purposes or where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

6.8 Business transfers

If our business or a relevant part of it is reorganised, transferred, or acquired, personal information may be transferred as part of that process where lawful and subject to appropriate protections.

We do not intend to sell customers’ personal information as a standalone commercial product. Any other disclosure or use must have an appropriate legal basis.

10. Passport, Visa, and Other Sensitive Documents

Passport and visa documents require particular care because they can contain information that may be misused if disclosed improperly. When you submit such documents to us:

  • We will use them for the relevant booking, visa assistance, or other clearly identified purpose.
  • We will seek to restrict access to people and service providers who need the information for that purpose.
  • We may share the documents with relevant providers or authorities where necessary and lawful.
  • We will retain them according to our retention practices and applicable legal requirements.
  • We will not knowingly ask you to upload unrelated documents without a legitimate reason.

Please ensure that you are authorised to provide personal information about other travellers. Where required, you should inform those travellers about the disclosure and obtain any necessary permission.

If you are submitting documents for a child or another person who cannot act independently, you should have the necessary authority to provide the information.
